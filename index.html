Margins are percentage points between the top two candidates. Maine and Alaska use ranked-choice voting, so agree on whether you're calling the first or final round. Picks stay sealed until you've both locked in; the seal is on the honor system. Senate balance counts the 65 seats not up this year (Angus King and Bernie Sanders with the Democrats) and the other 19 races as safe for the party holding them. Montana is scored on the winner only: its three-way race makes the margin too volatile, so it counts toward winners called and the Senate balance but not average miss or closer calls. Osborn and Bodnar count as independents, and a 50–50 Senate goes to the Republicans on the vice president's tiebreak.
